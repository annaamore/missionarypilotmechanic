About

Hello! My name is Anna Johnson. I am currently working towards becoming a missionary pilot. I will fly small planes into isolated areas to bring supplies and medical relief to those who need it. I will need my commercial pilot's license (250 flight hours) and my A&P certificate (mechanics cert).

In terms of flight training, I've been flying for a little over a year, and I have about 100 flight hours. I just completed my long solo cross country, from Centennial to Goodland KS to Pueblo and back, and I have been preparing intently for the checkride, which is scheduled for August 4th.

I'm currently working at The Pilot Shop, a store at Centennial Airport that sells tools and maps and gift items for pilots. I'm meeting a lot of interesting people and making a lot of money, saving for college!
