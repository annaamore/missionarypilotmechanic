About

Hello! My name is Anna Johnson. I am currently working towards becoming a missionary pilot. I will fly small planes into isolated areas to bring supplies and medical relief to those who need it. I will need my commercial pilot's license (250 flight hours) and my A&P certificate (mechanics cert).

In terms of flight training, I've been flying for a little over a year, and I have about 100 flight hours. I just failed my first check ride for private pilot's license :( and am looking to retake in the next few months. I will start my instrument training this summer.

I'm currently a maintenance student at Andrews University in Michigan, and for 40 hours a week I learn all about aircraft structures, electrical systems, and regulations.
